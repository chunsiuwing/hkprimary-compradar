# 📱 HKPrimary CompRadar (學界通) - Mobile App UI / Wireframe Specification

This document outlines the wireframes and screen layouts for the **HKPrimary CompRadar** mobile application (iOS & Android), designed for Hong Kong primary school teachers and parents.

---

## 🎨 Design System & Theme
* **Color Palette:** 
  * Primary: Deep Academic Blue (`#1B365D`) — Trust, professionalism, education.
  * Accent: Vibrant Emerald Green (`#00A86B`) — Success, active status, new items.
  * Alert: Coral Red (`#FF6B6B`) — Urgent deadlines (< 3 days).
  * Background: Soft Off-White (`#F7F9FC`).
* **Typography:** Traditional Chinese (Traditional characters as primary script for HK context), clean sans-serif.

## ✨ Key UX Features for HK Users
1. **Bilingual & Traditional Chinese First:** All titles and dates match Hong Kong conventions (e.g., 小四至小六, 全免, OEA證書).
2. **Urgency Indicators:** Color-coded countdown badges (`🔴` red for <3 days, `🟡` yellow for <2 weeks).
3. **One-Click Calendar & WhatsApp Sharing:** Teachers can instantly sync dates to their school calendar or share formatted summaries with colleague WhatsApp groups.
